// how it works

🎙️ Record

Tap to rip. An on-device DSP measures loudness, duration, bass depth and ripples in real time. Nothing leaves your phone until you post it.

📊 Score

Get a 0–100 Rip Score, normalized server-side so every microphone is judged fairly. 90+ is LEGENDARY.

👑 Rule

Your crew rates every clip with stars and emoji verdicts. Climb the daily best-rip board and the weekly crown. Invite-only, never public.

// the ladder

Every rip earns a grade. Scoring is relative — for entertainment, not calibrated SPL — but the leaderboard doesn't lie.

  • 90+Legendary
  • 80–89Certified Banger
  • 65–79Solid Rip
  • 50–64Respectable
  • 0–49A Mere Whisper
