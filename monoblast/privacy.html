Monoblast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

Monoblast (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a single-player block-puzzle game. This policy explains what data the app handles and the third-party services it uses. We keep it short because the app collects very little.

1. No account, minimal data

The app has no login or user accounts. Your game progress — high scores, coins, owned cosmetic skins, daily-challenge streak, and settings — is stored only on your device (in iOS UserDefaults). We do not collect, transmit, or sell this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads via Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s SDK may collect device information and identifiers (such as the Advertising Identifier, IDFA). With your permission it may use these for personalized ads; without permission, ads are non-personalized.

See Google’s policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses data.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the app asks for permission to track via Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the app serves non-personalized ads and does not use the IDFA for tracking. You can change this any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

The optional “Remove Ads” purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your name, card, or payment details — only whether the purchase is active, so we can hide ads.

5. Game Center (Apple)

If you sign in to Game Center, the app submits your scores to Apple’s leaderboards. Game Center is operated by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. Leaderboards are optional — declining Game Center simply hides them.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your choices

  • Decline tracking in the ATT prompt or iOS Tracking settings.
  • Buy “Remove Ads” to stop banner and interstitial ads.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
