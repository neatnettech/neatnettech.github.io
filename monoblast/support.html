How to play

  • Drag a piece from your tray onto the 8×8 board, wherever it fits.
  • Fill any full row or column and it clears instantly.
  • Clear lines on back-to-back moves to build a combo and multiply your score.
  • It's game over when none of your three pieces fit.

Common questions

How do I remove ads? Settings → Remove ads (a one-time purchase). Use Restore purchases on a new device.

What are coins for? Coins are earned by playing and spent on cosmetic skins. They never affect gameplay — skins are cosmetic only.

Is there an account? No. Your progress is stored on your device. Sign in to Game Center for leaderboards (optional).

I'm color-blind. Settings → Color-blind patterns adds a distinct shape inside every block, on any skin.

How does the daily streak work? Complete the Daily Challenge each day to grow your streak. Miss a day and it resets.
